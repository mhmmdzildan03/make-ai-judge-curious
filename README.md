A stdlib-only Python program that writes one short text: every dawn, Tomas rows an empty chair across a still river, sets it facing the water on the far bank, and pours a second cup of tea beside his own — for someone he never names, who likes to arrive unhurried and has never once been late. A calm, concrete little scene of 260 characters, written to leave the reader quietly curious about who the chair is for. Deterministic, no network.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 260 characters).
